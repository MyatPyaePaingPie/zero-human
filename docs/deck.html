Buy evidence

Cheapest sufficient source: the room, recruited humans via Terac, Replay QA clicking the live product.

What it learned todayfrom receipts, not assertion

Every verdict settles against humans.

Model evaluators commit first; humans settle; the gap is scored. The company re-prices its evidence sources from its own settled jobs — deduped per job, and failing closed to priors until n ≥ 10.

—jobs settled by humans —verdicts humans overturned —before → after, measured

The before/after is pre-registered: the first verdict's hash locks before the humans answer, and repeat raters are refused. "Our feedback improved them" is a receipt here, not a claim.
